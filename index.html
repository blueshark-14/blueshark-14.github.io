Applying for Fall 2027 Ph.D. programs

I build real-time smart home interfaces, and I want to study how people interact with them, and how AI can adapt them to the person using them.

I am a software engineer with more than three years at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh. I led the architecture and delivery of SmartThings Routine Creation & Editing on Windows, supported a Unity-based 3D home map, and worked on a VR proof of concept for controlling home appliances. These projects made me curious about the questions behind the interface: how do users form intent, where do they get stuck, and how can models of user behavior make immersive and accessible systems better?

My research background is in modeling under uncertainty. My B.Sc. thesis on fuzzy inference systems led to a journal article in Heliyon and a Springer conference paper. I am now looking to move into technical HCI, AR/VR, and AI/ML for interactive systems.
