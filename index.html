Biography

About Me

I am a Senior Software Engineer with over three years of experience building Windows platform features at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh, where I led the end-to-end delivery of SmartThings Routine Creation & Editing on Windows — from architecture through launch — reaching millions of users. My technical focus is on real-time UI, asynchronous data pipelines, and cross-platform collaboration using C#, .NET, WPF, WinUI 3, and MVVM.


Beyond industry work, I am an active researcher. I hold a B.Sc. in CSE from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology, with a thesis on fuzzy inference systems for chemical risk evaluation. I have one peer-reviewed journal article and one conference paper, with a patent contribution filed during my time at Samsung. I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in the USA and UK to pursue research at the intersection of software systems, AI, and human-computer interaction.
